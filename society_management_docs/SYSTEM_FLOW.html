Complete end-to-end operational blueprint, business logic specifications, role-based authority rules, and automated workflows for society governance, visitor security, and maintenance billing.

🏢 Phase 1: Society & President Onboarding & Verification

📊 View Diagram #2 →

Step 1.1: Society Self-Registration by President / Secretary

  1. Registration Action: Submit online registration form.
  2. Payload Collected:
    • President Personal Credentials: Full Name, Mobile Number, Email, Password, Designation (President / Secretary / Chairman).
    • Society Profile: Society Name, Address, City, State, Pincode, Latitude / Longitude (Geofence), Registration Number (Cooperative Society / RWA No.), Optional GST & PAN.
    • Verification Documents: Society Registration Certificate upload (PDF/Image) and President ID Proof (Aadhaar / PAN).
  3. Initial System State:
    • societies.status = 'pending_verification'
    • users.status = 'pending'
    • user_societies.status = 'pending'
    • President cannot access society management features until verified.

Step 1.2: Platform Backend Verification (Super Admin)

  1. Super Admin Review: Super Admin logs into the Platform Management Portal to review uploaded legal documents and registration data.
  2. Action - Approve:
    • societies.status transitions to 'pending_subscription' (Verified, awaiting SaaS payment).
    • users.status becomes 'active' and user_societies.status becomes 'approved'.
    • Spatie role society-admin is assigned.
    • Automated approval email & SMS dispatched to President.
  3. Action - Reject:
    • societies.status updated to 'rejected' with reason note. Email sent back with re-upload instructions.

Step 1.3: SaaS Subscription Plan Selection & Payment Flow

📦
Basic Plan
Resident directory, notice broadcasts, basic maintenance billing, and member profiles.
🛡️
Standard Plan
Includes Gate Security App, Visitor logs, Domestic staff tracking, and Helpdesk complaints.
💎
Premium Plan
Advanced Double-Entry Accounting, Bank Reconciliation, Amenity Booking, and Analytics.
✨
Instant SaaS Activation: Once payment succeeds via Razorpay / Stripe (or 14-day trial selected), a record is created in society_subscriptions and societies.status transitions to 'active'.

Step 1.4: Society Physical Layout Configuration

  • Blocks / Towers / Wings: Configure layout (e.g., Tower A, Tower B, Wing 1).
  • Flats / Units: Add flats with details (e.g., Flat 101, 102; Type: 2BHK, 3BHK; Sq. Ft.).
  • Optional Unique Flat Identifier (unique_flat_code` / flat_uid): Custom reference code optionally set per flat (e.g., SOC-A-101).
  • Society Hierarchy Ready: Towers, Wings, and Flats are configured, ready for the President/Admin to register owners, tenants, and committee members!

👥 Phase 2: Centralized User Registration, Family Setup & RBAC (President/Admin Driven)

📊 View Diagram #3 & #4 →

Step 2.1: Centralized User Account Creation by President / Admin

🛡️
Centralized President & Admin Onboarding: In this platform, user accounts are not self-registered by unverified public users. To ensure complete security, verified occupancy, and data privacy, all user accounts are registered and created directly by the President or authorized Admin / Executive Committee Members.
Account Type Being Created Who Has Authority to Register / Create? Credentials & Initial Activation
Executive Committee Member President ONLY (role:president) Instant activation; Spatie role assigned (Secretary/Treasurer/VP); Welcome credentials sent
Flat Owner Account President OR Authorized Executive Committee Members Linked to flat; marked owner_occupied or rented; instant approval
Tenant Account President OR Authorized Executive Committee Members Tagged explicitly with role:resident-tenant; linked to rented flat
Security Guard Account President OR Authorized Executive Committee Members Assigned gate number and guard station credentials
Maintenance Staff Account President OR Authorized Executive Committee Members Assigned trade category (Electrician, Plumber) and society service access

Step 2.1.1: Adding Family Member Details & Household Configuration

During or immediately following account creation, the President or Admin can configure the complete household profile for the flat, which residents can also later update in their own app:

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Family Member Enrollment
President / Admin adds family members (Spouse, Children, Elderly Parents) with name, relationship, phone number, and optional independent app access toggle.
🚗
Vehicle & Parking Assignment
President / Admin enters authorized 2-wheelers / 4-wheelers, license plate numbers, and designated parking slot numbers for automatic gate security clearance.
🔑
Owner vs. Tenant Separation
For rented flats, President / Admin creates separate logins: Tenant receives daily gate & service alerts; Owner retains governance & ledger oversight.

Step 2.3: Phone Number Login, Role Selection Popup & Multi-Society Context Switching

When an individual logs in using their registered Phone Number, the system detects all linked profiles, flats, and roles across societies (e.g., an individual who is both a Secretary and an Owner, or owns flats in two different buildings).

📱
1. Phone Number Login
User enters registered mobile number + password or SMS OTP. The API validates credentials and queries all approved memberships in user_societies.
🪟
2. Role Selection Popup Modal
If the phone number holds multiple roles or flats, a clean popup modal appears immediately: user selects whether to enter as Secretary, Flat Owner, or Tenant.
🛡️
3. Scoped Request Headers
Selected context is attached to subsequent API requests via X-Society-Id, X-Active-Role, and X-Flat-Id, automatically scoping bills, visitor passes, and notices.
🔄
4. 1-Tap In-App Switcher
Users never need to log out. Tapping "Switch Role / Flat" in the app header reopens the selection popup modal to toggle personas instantly.

🛡️ Phase 3: Gate Security & Visitor Operations

📊 View Diagram #5 →

Step 3.1: Flat-Specific Visitors (Guests, Deliveries & Cabs)

  1. Visitor Arrival: Guest, courier, or cab arrives at the gate for a specific flat.
  2. Guard Input:
    • Live Photo: Guard snaps a photo with the Guard Mobile App camera.
    • Target Flat Number: Selected from the society flat directory.
    • Details: Name, Mobile Number, optional Vehicle Number, and Visitor Type.
  3. Automated Instant Notification: Backend pushes an immediate alert with visitor photo to the resident's mobile phone: "Your visitor [Name] has arrived at the gate."

Step 3.2: Maintenance Staff & Contractors (Multi-Flat Access)

🔧
Frictionless Entry for Service Technicians: Because maintenance contractors (plumbers, electricians, lift technicians) service multiple flats:
  • NO Photo Required: Prevents gate bottlenecks.
  • NO Single Flat Constraint: Granted society-wide access.
  • Guard Inputs: Worker Name, Phone, and Trade Category tag (e.g., Electrician, Plumber, Lift AMC).

Step 3.3: Digital Gate Exit & Live Premises Dashboard

The guard app maintains a real-time list of all visitors and service workers currently inside the premises. 1-tap "Exit" logs the exact departure timestamp for a complete digital audit trail.

💳 Phase 4: Financial Accounting & Maintenance Cost Collection

📊 View Diagram #6 →

Step 4.1: Itemized Maintenance Demand Generation

  • Configurable Rules: Fixed monthly rate (e.g. ₹3,500/flat) OR Area-based rate (e.g. 1,250 sqft × ₹3/sqft = ₹3,750).
  • Itemized Line Items: Base maintenance, Sinking/Reserve fund, Water/Electricity readings, Reserved parking bay, and Carried forward arrears.
  • Automated Dispatch: Invoices generated on the 1st of every month with push alerts and WhatsApp notifications.

Step 4.2: In-App Payment Modes Available to Every Resident

⚡
Option A: Instant Online Payment
Pay via UPI (GPay/PhonePe), Credit/Debit Card, or NetBanking via Razorpay / Stripe. Instant auto-reconciliation and official receipt with QR.
🏦
Option B: Direct Bank / UPI Transfer (Zero Fee)
Resident transfers funds directly to Society Bank Account or scans Dynamic UPI QR, submits 12-digit UTR, and uploads receipt screenshot.
💵
Option C: Cash / Cheque Offline Entry
Accountant manually records cash or cheque deposit entries (cheque number, bank name, deposit date) directly into the Admin portal.

Step 4.4: Dynamic Late Fee Calculation & Accountant Re-Generate Action

⏱️
1. Dynamic Late Fee Calculation on Fetch (Resident App):
No background penalty cron jobs. The late fee is calculated on-the-fly when the resident fetches their bill in the app:
  • If current_date <= due_date: Late fee is ₹0.
  • If current_date > due_date: Calculates late fee in real time based on overdue days.
  • Total Payable = Base Maintenance + Dynamically Calculated Late Fee.
🔄
2. Accountant Re-Generate Maintenance Action Button:
In the Accountant Admin Portal, every invoice has an explicit action button:
🔄 [ Re-Generate / Recalculate Maintenance ]
What this button does:
  • Recalculates the exact late fee and latest dues up to the current date.
  • Permanently locks and saves the finalized late fee into invoices.late_fee_amount and updates the flat ledger before issuing demand letters.

🎫 Phase 5: Grievance & Complaint Helpdesk Flow

📊 View Diagram #7 →

Step 5.1: Complaint Submission (Any Society Member)

Any registered member (Owners, Tenants, Guards, Executive Members) can file complaints across categories (Plumbing, Electrical, Lift, Water, Noise, Garbage) with photos and priority levels.

Step 5.2: Shared Committee Visibility & Action Routing

All new tickets appear simultaneously in the dashboards of both the President and all Executive Committee Members. Each ticket has 4 powerful routing actions:

👤
Forward to Specific Executive
Assigns ticket to a designated committee lead (e.g. Secretary forwards water issue to Water Committee Lead).
👑
Forward / Escalate to President
Escalates severe structural, security, or legal complaints directly to the President for policy resolution.
🛠️
Assign to Maintenance Staff
Dispatches work order directly to society electrician, plumber, or external AMC agency technician.
✋
Self-Assign / Take Ownership
The logged-in President or Executive Member takes personal responsibility for resolving the ticket.

🏊 Phase 6: Amenity & Facility Booking Flow

Facility Management
  1. Browse: Residents view bookable amenities (Clubhouse, Tennis Court, Banquet Hall, Party Lawn).
  2. Slot Selection: Select date and available time slot with automatic double-booking collision prevention.
  3. Fee & Deposit: Calculates hourly fee + refundable security deposit.
  4. Confirmation: Automatically charges booking fee to resident flat ledger and alerts gate security.
